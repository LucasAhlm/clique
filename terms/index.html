Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version. Be 18 or older, be yourself, and be kind. We do not tolerate harassment, hate, sexual content or anything abusive. Report anyone who breaks these rules; we look at every report within 24 hours and remove people who break them.

1. Who these terms are between

These terms are an agreement between you and Lucas Ahlm, who runs Clique ("we", "us"). By creating an account you agree to them and to our privacy policy. If you don't agree, please don't use Clique.

2. Who can use Clique

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You need your own phone number. One account per person.
  • You may not use Clique if we have removed you before.

3. The rules

Clique is for meeting new people in person. To keep it safe and friendly, you agree not to:

  • harass, bully, threaten or intimidate anyone, on Clique or when you meet
  • post hateful content about anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, sexuality or other part of who they are
  • post nudity, sexual content or violent content
  • pretend to be someone else, use photos that aren't you or your group, or make fake groups
  • use Clique to sell, advertise, recruit or ask for money
  • share someone else's private information without their permission
  • do anything illegal, or encourage others to
  • try to break, overload, scrape or get around the limits of the app

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You are responsible for what you and your group post and for how you behave when you meet.

4. Reporting, blocking and removal

You can block or report a group from your chat with them at any time. We review every report within 24 hours. If someone has broken these rules, we may remove their content, remove them from their group, or delete their account and stop their phone number from signing up again. We may also act without a report when we see a clear breach.

5. Meeting up safely

Clique helps groups meet, but we don't check who people are beyond their phone number, and we are not there when you meet. Meet in public places, tell someone where you're going, and leave if you feel unsafe. In an emergency, call 112.

6. Your content

Your photos, profile and messages stay yours. You give us permission to store them and show them to other people in the app, as described in the privacy policy, for as long as they are on Clique. Only post things you have the right to share.

7. Clique+ and payments

Clique is free to use. If we add paid features later, they will be bought through Apple, and we will say clearly what they cost before you pay.

8. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. We may close accounts that break these terms.

We work to keep Clique running and safe, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. We are not responsible for how other people behave, on Clique or when you meet. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under consumer law.

10. Apple

These terms are between you and us, not Apple. Apple is not responsible for Clique or its content, and has no obligation to provide support for it. Apple and its subsidiaries may enforce these terms as a third-party beneficiary.

11. Changes and law

If we change these terms, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before big changes take effect. Swedish law applies to these terms.

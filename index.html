How it works

Friends are better in groups.

Going out is easier when nobody has to go first. On Clique, your whole group decides together, so every match is one you all want.

1

Make your Clique

Invite up to five friends with a code, or start on your own. Add a group photo, what you're up for, and the city you're in.

2

Swipe together

Everyone swipes on the groups nearby. A yes is only sent when enough of your group says yes too.

3

Match and meet up

When two groups like each other, you share a chat, vote on a plan and pick a time. Then go meet.

Made for real life

New in town, or just want new faces?

Visiting for a few nights, moved here last month, or lived here forever: Clique shows who is around and what they're into, from padel and board games to dinner and concerts.

  • Up for tonightLet groups know you're free tonight and find others who are too.

  • Dating, only if you want itClique is about friends first. Dating is something you switch on.

Safety

Real people, and you stay in control.

Meeting strangers should feel easy, not risky. These are built in from day one.

✓

Verified numbers

Every member signs in with their own phone number. No fake groups, and everyone is 18 or older.

⌖

Only your city is shared

Your exact location never leaves your phone. Other groups only see which city you're in.

!

Block and report

Block a group in one tap and they disappear for you, and you for them. Reports go straight to us.

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version. We only collect what Clique needs to work. Your exact location never leaves your phone. We never sell your data or use it for ads. You can delete your account and data yourself, any time, in the app.

Who we are

Clique is an app where friend groups, or people on their own, meet other groups nearby. Clique is run by Lucas Ahlm, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

A contact address for questions about your data will be added here before Clique opens to the public. We answer within 30 days.

What other people see

Other signed-in people in the same city can see your group card: your group's name, bio, photos, activities, prompts and city, and each member's first name, age, country flag, photos and the profile details they filled in.

  • Your date of birth and phone number are never shown. Only your age is, and you can hide it.
  • Chats are only seen by the members of the two matched groups.
  • Reports are only seen by us, never by the group you reported.
  • A group you block no longer sees you, and you no longer see them.

Location

Your exact location never leaves your phone. If you allow location, the app reads a rough position on your phone, works out which Clique city you are in, and only that city name is sent to us.

  • Clique only uses location while you are using the app, never in the background.
  • It uses approximate location by default.
  • You can say no and pick your city yourself, or turn location off any time in iPhone Settings → Apps → Clique → Location.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete it yourself at any time in Settings → Delete account, and it happens straight away.

When you delete your account, we delete:

  • your phone number, date of birth, profile and photos
  • your chat messages, votes, meetup answers and limits
  • your group, its photos and its matches, if you were the last person in it

What stays:

  • If others are still in your group, the group and its matches stay with them.
  • Reports you made stay, without your name, so we can still act on them.
  • If we remove an account for breaking our terms of use, we keep a one-way scrambled copy (a hash) of its phone number, so the same number can't sign up again. We also keep the report and what we decided.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's automatic backups disappear when those backups expire.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, get a copy, or object to how we use it. Most of it you can see and change in the app. For the rest, use the contact address above once it is added. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Age limit

Clique is only for people aged 18 and over. Sign-up stops anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before big changes take effect.

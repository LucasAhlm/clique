Answers to the most common questions about Clique.

Getting started

How do I sign up?

Open Clique, tap Continue with phone and enter the code we text you. Then add your name, date of birth and a few details, and either start a group, join one with an invite code, or start on your own.

How do I get my friends into my group?

Go to the Group tab and tap Invite friends to make it a group (or Invite more friends). Share the code; your friends enter it while signing up. A group can have up to six people.

Can I use Clique on my own?

Yes. Pick On my own when you sign up. You'll match with groups and other people who are solo, and you can invite friends later to turn it into a group.

Which cities is Clique in?

Stockholm, Lisbon and Barcelona for now. You can tap Use my location, or pick your city yourself.

Swiping and matching

Why didn't my yes go through?

In a group, a yes is only sent once enough of your group has swiped yes on the same group. Your friends will see it waiting for them in Discover.

What happens when we match?

Both groups get a shared chat with a few plan ideas. Vote on one, or suggest your own, and confirm it once both groups have voted.

Is there a limit on swipes?

Free accounts get 20 swipes and one undo a day. The count starts again each day.

Safety and privacy

How do I block or report a group?

Open your chat with them and choose Report or Block. A blocked group no longer sees you, and you no longer see them. Reports come straight to us.

Who can see where I am?

Only the city you're in is shared. Your exact location never leaves your phone. Read more in our privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to You → Settings → Delete account. Your profile, photos and messages are deleted straight away, and your group too if you were the last one in it. This can't be undone.

Still need help? A contact address is coming before Clique opens to the public. If a group makes you feel unsafe, use Report in your chat with them and it comes straight to us.
